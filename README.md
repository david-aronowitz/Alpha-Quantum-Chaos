# Quantum Chaos in Quantum Mechanics 🌌⚛️

Research exploring the connection between classical chaos theory and quantum mechanics principles by constructing a 2D quantum system with a potential simulating a black hole.

---

## 📌 Overview & Methodology

This study investigated system behavior to evaluate whether it displays characteristics of quantum chaos.

The research utilized two forms of the Schrödinger equation:
* ⏳ **Time-Dependent Equation:** Used to explore system dynamics, computed using the **Split-Step Fourier Method**.
* 🎯 **Time-Independent Equation:** Used to compute the system's eigenstates and energy levels.

Energy level spacing histograms were constructed and compared against well-known theoretical distributions:
* **Poisson**
* **Gaussian Orthogonal Ensemble (GOE)**
* **Gaussian Unitary Ensemble (GUE)**

---

## 💡 Results & Key Findings

* The results showed that the system is **non-integrable** (not "non-chaotic"), but also **does not show distinct signs of quantum chaos**.
* This finding supports the concept that quantum chaos is not a binary property, but rather exists on a **continuous spectrum of chaotic degrees**.
